FROM A THOUGHT TO A RECORD

More than the moment
you pressed record.

Capture, transcribe, review and organize. Export your words into files that belong in your own collection.

Quick notes & all-day recording

Catch the thought while it is here.

Double-tap on the App’s home screen, or use a configured iPhone Back Tap, to start a quick recording; repeat to stop. All-day recording saves automatic 10, 20 or 30-minute segments, keeping saved segments if recording is interrupted.

Transcripts & original audio

Read the words. Hear them again.

Transcribe a full day or selected portions on your phone, or use a cloud model you configure. Retry an individual segment, tap a transcript passage to replay its audio, and keep the text even after clearing the audio.

Daily review

See your day from different angles.

Choose a daily review, full review, time-period details or a timeline. Move between today, the previous or next day, or review the past week or month.

Actionable prompts

Give your records a next step.

Turn today’s records into a prompt you can copy into any AI tool you choose. Use focused summaries and your own organization templates in My Templates.

Knowledge base

Bring your earlier words along.

Import Markdown or plain-text files. Search and review earlier writing alongside what you record today.

Mindfulness cards

Leave a reminder for yourself.

Receive a daily reminder card, called “善念与你” in the App. Look back at mood trends over a period of time.

Favorites & highlights

Keep the lines that matter.

Save meaningful sentences to your favorites. Let them grow into your own collection of highlights.

Export & archive

Keep your own files.

Export a full backup, one day’s original Markdown or transcripts. Text is automatically archived into folders by day, in a file structure you can take with you.

A DAY WITH NIANJI

At the pace of your life.

One possible routine. You decide when to start recording and what is worth keeping.

Morning

Catch a passing thought.

Record an idea with a quick note. If your phone is not at hand, record independently on Apple Watch.

Daytime

Make room for longer thoughts.

Start all-day recording when you need it. Audio saves in segments; return to a transcript passage to hear the original words.

Evening

Gather the day.

Read the daily review, favorite a sentence, generate a prompt or export the day’s Markdown into your own files.

DATA & PRIVACY

Your words stay yours.

Your recordings and text are stored on your own phone. We have no servers receiving your recordings or text.

Full privacy policy (中文)

Local storageKeep and manage your recordings, transcripts and personal material on your own device.

Cloud processing is optionalYou configure the provider. API keys are stored in this device’s Keychain. Content needed for cloud processing goes to the provider you choose.

No data sales or profilingWe do not sell your personal data or build user profiles. Nianji contains no advertising.
